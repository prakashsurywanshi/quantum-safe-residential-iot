Key Points — for editor reference
Manuscript: A Sustainable Hybrid QKD–PQC Security Architecture for
            Quantum-Resilient Residential IoT
Author: Prakash Suryawanshi (Qodeigence Infocircle Private Limited)
Artifact: https://github.com/prakashsurywanshi/quantum-safe-residential-iot
          (branch ieee-submission, tag ieee-v1.0)

1. Gateway-centric hybrid QKD–PQC architecture (QHSG) isolates asymmetric
   post-quantum key management from high-volume leaf-node data traffic, keeping
   constrained IoT endpoints symmetric-only.

2. Hardware-grounded security–sustainability model: IEEE 802.15.4 physical-layer
   radio constants (TX 22 mW, RX 21 mW at 250 kbps), ARM Cortex-M4 cycle counts,
   and 6LoWPAN fragmentation as explicit design parameters.

3. Direct leaf-node PQC raises endpoint key-negotiation energy by +108.4%
   (328.46 mJ vs 157.63 mJ at N=100) because the 2272-byte ML-KEM-768 handshake
   fragments into 24 IEEE 802.15.4 frames.

4. The proposed hybrid architecture reduces endpoint key-management energy by
   −96.8% vs standalone PQC and −93.3% vs classical ECDH — a sustainable path to
   post-quantum resilience for battery-driven smart homes.

5. Protocol-level validation in executed Contiki-NG/COOJA across seven payload
   sizes (64–2272 B); measured frame counts (1, 2, 3, 6, 11, 13, 24) match the
   closed form ceil((payload+12)/96) exactly.

6. Formal verification in ProVerif 2.05 of key-lifecycle secrecy and
   authentication, single-use refresh, endpoint isolation, gateway-compromise
   forward secrecy, key independence, replay under loss, and impersonation.

7. Fully reproducible: public tagged artifact with one run script per evidence
   stream, including the Contiki-NG patch needed for the 2272-byte handshake.
